Good to know

Common questions from landlords

How long does approval take?

Most listings are reviewed and published within 24 hours. We check the address and details so renters can trust what they see.

Who sees my contact details?

Only renters who send you a viewing request. Your street address stays private until you choose to share it.

Is it really free?

Yes. Listing, editing and receiving enquiries are free. We don't take commission on your rent — now or later.
